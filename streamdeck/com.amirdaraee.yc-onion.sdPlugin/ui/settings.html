Auto uses the remembered device on macOS or the strongest nearby match on Windows.

The light must be physically powered on. Color: hue 0=red, 60=yellow, 120=green, 180=cyan, 240=blue, 300=magenta.

Each press cycles through red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple, and magenta.

Advanced commands are protocol-recovered, not hardware-tested. A motion packet can move a slider or gimbal. Set the exact device name/UUID and test Stop before movement.
